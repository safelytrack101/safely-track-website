SafetyCulture Alternative for Consulting Firms

Why not SafetyCulture / Mitti?

SafetyCulture, now rebranded as Mitti, is a strong inspection app for teams checking their own operations. A consulting firm does something different: it produces a deliverable for many clients, proves the fixes, and bills the hours. This page is an honest look at where SafetyCulture fits that job and where it leaves the work to Word, email, and spreadsheets.

Credit where it's due

What SafetyCulture does well.

If your firm needs digital checklists, SafetyCulture is a reasonable place to start, and many safety professionals already know it.

A huge template library

Thousands of public checklists and inspection templates, plus an AI assistant to create new ones.

A free plan

Up to 10 seats and 5 active templates at no cost. Premium is listed at $24 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 monthly.

Broad operations coverage

Inspections, issues and tasks, training, assets, contractor management, incident investigations, and sensors.

Branded reports and guests

The Premium plan lists custom branded reports and free guest seats.

Where consulting firms outgrow it

The checklist gets done. The firm's work still happens elsewhere.

Mitti describes itself as built for frontline teams in manufacturing, hospitality, construction, retail, and facilities. That is an operator inspecting its own sites. For a consulting firm, the inspection is the start of the job, not the end of it. These are the gaps firms most often describe to us.

Often left outside the inspection app
  • ✕ Rewriting the export into each client's own report format
  • ✕ Reading a week of dailies to write the client summary by hand
  • ✕ Chasing client workers and subs by phone to confirm fixes
  • ✕ Hours, invoices, and margin per client in spreadsheets and QuickBooks
  • ✕ Per-seat cost that grows with every consultant you hire
How Safely Track handles it
  • ✓ Templates built from each client's forms, output as their PDF with your logo
  • ✓ The week's dailies draft the client summary; the consultant edits and sends
  • ✓ Proof of fix by text or email link, closed with a photo and timestamp, no login
  • ✓ Timesheets, invoicing, and margin per client in the same system
  • ✓ Consultant credentials matched to what each client requires

Which to choose

An honest recommendation.

Stay with SafetyCulture if

  • You mostly need checklists and your clients accept a standard export
  • You are a solo consultant and the free plan covers you
  • Billing, credentials, and client summaries are already handled well elsewhere

Look at Safely Track if

  • Your consultants spend hours turning field notes into client deliverables
  • Each client wants its report its own way
  • Closeout proof, credentials, and hours live in three other systems

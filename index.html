Questions

What consulting firms ask first.

Who is Safely Track for?

Safety and EHS consulting firms that serve many clients: audits, training, site safety staffing and outsourced safety programs. Loss control teams and in-house safety teams use the field reporting and closeout parts on their own.

How is it different from regular EHS software?

Regular EHS software assumes one employer with one safety department. A consulting firm has many clients, each with its own report format, credential requirements, contacts and invoice. Safely Track keeps each client separate, gives the firm one view across all of them, and covers staffing, timesheets and billing too.

Can we use our own report templates?

Yes. Send the forms you use today and they are built as templates, with your fields, issue types and risk tiers, and a report PDF that looks like the one your client already receives.

How long does setup take?

Templates are built from your existing forms, so the field team sees familiar paperwork on day one. When you want a change and cannot make it yourself, we usually make it within a day. One consulting firm replaced its long-standing EHS platform within three weeks of going live.

Do client workers or subcontractors need an account?

No. The person responsible for a fix gets a text or email with a link, replies with a photo, and the record closes with a timestamp. No app download or login on their end.

How is it priced?

Pricing is not published. It is quoted per firm after a demo on your own reports. There is no free plan, but you can start with a pilot on a live client.

The Problem

None of it is missing. It's just scattered.

The credential that decides who can staff a job lives in a spreadsheet. The photos that prove a hazard got fixed live on somebody's phone. The hours that decide your margin live in an inbox until someone reconstructs them on Friday. Every piece exists, and none of it is anywhere the next step can reach it. That's not a field problem. It's an operations problem.

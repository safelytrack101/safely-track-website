Why firms compare

Why consulting firms look at alternatives.

When a safety consulting firm starts comparing software, it is usually for one of three reasons, whatever tool it uses today.

Cost and terms that grow with the firm

Subscriptions priced by headcount or company size, renewed annually, cost more with every consultant you hire, while the client work is what you actually sell.

Built for one company's program

Most safety platforms are designed around one employer running its own safety program. A firm serves many clients, each with its own report format, contacts and requirements.

The back office lives in spreadsheets

The inspection gets done in the app, but hours, invoices and margin per client are still in spreadsheets and QuickBooks.

The report is still rebuilt by hand

Field notes still get rewritten into each client's deliverable and weekly summary, which is where most consultant hours go.

Credit where it's due

What Safety Reports does well.

Built with consultants in mind

It names loss control representatives and third-party safety consultants as core users, with a company-branded cover letter and centralized client data.

Ready-made content

Pre-loaded checklists, uploads of your own, and more than 600 toolbox talks.

Offline mobile apps

Inspections, incidents, training, forms, JSAs and OSHA log generation, with mobile apps that work offline at remote sites.

No long-term contracts

Its site says there are no startup fees and no long-term contracts, with free web training and support for subscribers.

Questions

What firms comparing Safety Reports ask.

Who owns Safety Reports?

Safety Reports says it was acquired by Align Technologies, formerly ToolWatch, in 2022. The product is now branded AlignOps SafetyReports.

Is Safety Reports a good fit for a safety consulting firm?

It was built with third-party consultants and loss control reps in mind, so often yes for inspections and checklists. Firms tend to look further when they want AI-drafted reports, each client's full report format, weekly client summaries, and hours and billing per client in the same place.

Does Safety Reports publish its pricing?

No. Its pricing page lists Pro and Premium plans with contact for pricing. Its site says there are no startup fees and no long-term contracts.

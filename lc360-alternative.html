LC360 / Majesco Loss Control

An LC360 alternative for teams who want shorter, faster loss control reports

LC360, now sold as Majesco Loss Control, is a core system for P&C carriers. Safely Track runs the survey itself: requests in a queue by due date, a route for the day, a confirmation text to the site contact, and a report drafted in your format from what the inspector said and photographed, before they are back in the car.

Survey management

The survey program, without the carrier suite.

LC360 sells itself as the survey management system. Safely Track manages the survey too, built for the firms and teams that do the surveys:

Request queue by due date

Survey requests land in one queue with the nearest due date on top. Assign each to an inspector and a time; two schedulers can never book the same one twice.

Due dates and overdue

Every survey carries its due date. One view shows what is overdue, what is due this week and your on-time rate.

Routes and confirmations

Each inspector's day is ordered to finish earliest, with visit lengths learned from past surveys. The site contact confirms by text, and reminders go out before the visit.

Review, then closeout

Reports wait in a review queue before they reach the client. The policyholder then closes each recommendation from a text link with a photo, no login.

What LC360 has that we don't.

Two things, both for carriers managing a whole book: embedded property risk insight that picks which locations get surveyed, and portfolio and reinsurance analysis across books and regions. Majesco also lists automatic workload balancing across vendor networks; in Safely Track a scheduler assigns from the queue. If you are a carrier buying a core system for underwriting and reinsurance, look at LC360. If you are the people doing the surveys, it is a lot of system to carry.

Where Safely Track fits

The report is the work.

A survey template that has grown for years produces a report that is long because every section gets filled. Underwriters skim for the hazards and recommendations. Safely Track starts from your template, but the draft comes from what the inspector actually photographed and described, so each finding sits in the right section with its recommendation next to it.

The typical write-up
  • ✕ Notes and photos held until the inspector is back at a desk
  • ✕ Every template section filled by hand, relevant or not
  • ✕ Recommendation language retyped for each hazard
  • ✕ Policyholder follow-up by email with no proof of the fix
With Safely Track
  • ✓ Narrate the building as you walk it and tick the photos that show a hazard
  • ✓ Construction, occupancy and conditions land in your template's sections
  • ✓ Standard recommendation language drafted per hazard, for review
  • ✓ Policyholder closes the recommendation from a text link with a photo

How voice and photos feed the report.

Open a report and pick your survey template. Choose the policyholder and the property, drop in the survey photos, and talk through the building in the order you already inspect it. Hit generate: the voice memo and photos become a draft in your template, and each hazard gets standard recommendation language your underwriters already expect. The inspector reviews, edits and submits. You can watch this in the loss control demo video.

Which to choose

Which one fits.

Choose LC360 if

  • You are a carrier buying a core system for underwriting and reinsurance
  • You need property risk scoring to pick which locations get surveyed
  • You balance work across a network of outside vendors

Look at Safely Track if

  • You do the surveys and want the queue, routes and reports in one place
  • Your inspectors spend longer writing the report than walking the site
  • You want recommendations closed with a photo, not chased by email
Questions

What loss control teams ask.

Is LC360 the same as Majesco Loss Control?

LossControl360 was built by Utilant, which Majesco acquired. Majesco now presents the product on its own site as Majesco Loss Control, alongside a field capture product called Guide Stream.

What does LC360 do that Safely Track doesn't?

Two things, both for carriers managing a whole book: embedded property risk insight that picks which locations get surveyed, and portfolio and reinsurance analysis. Majesco also lists automatic workload balancing across vendor networks. Survey intake, due dates, assignment, routes, visit confirmations, review, the report and recommendation follow-up are all in Safely Track.

Do I have to replace LC360 to use Safely Track?

No. Many firms run the whole survey in Safely Track. If a carrier client requires LC360, Safely Track runs alongside it: it handles the queue, the walk and the report, and the finished report goes wherever the carrier needs it.

Why are loss control reports so long?

Many survey templates grew one section at a time, and every section gets filled whether the underwriter needs it or not. Safely Track builds the report from your template but drafts only what the inspector saw and said, so findings and recommendations are easy to find.

Can inspectors dictate a loss control survey report?

Yes. In Safely Track the inspector talks through the building while walking it and adds photos. The voice memo and photos are drafted into the sections of your survey template, with recommendation language for each hazard, and the inspector reviews before sending.

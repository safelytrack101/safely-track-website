LC360 / Majesco Loss Control

An LC360 alternative for teams who want shorter, faster loss control reports

LC360, now presented as Majesco Loss Control, is a survey management platform built for P&C insurers. Credit where it's due

What LC360 does that Safely Track doesn't.

Majesco describes Loss Control as a cloud-native platform that helps P&C insurers organize survey information, property insight, recommendations and follow-up. On its product pages it lists:

Property risk insight

Embedded property risk insights to decide which locations and exposures warrant a survey in the first place.

Survey management at insurer scale

Automated assignment, reminders and follow-ups across field teams and vendors, with workload balancing for managers.

Field, digital and self-surveys

Standard templates across field, digital and self-surveys, plus Guide Stream for photos, video and responses collected in the field.

Portfolio and reinsurance analysis

Structured survey data aggregated across books, regions and classes to feed portfolio steering and reinsurance strategy.

Majesco also lists AI-assisted recommendations and turning field audio into structured data. If you are a carrier choosing a system of record for your whole survey program, those are real strengths, and Safely Track is not a replacement for that platform.

Where Safely Track fits

Long reports versus shorter, structured ones.

A survey template that has grown for years produces a report that is long because every section gets filled. Underwriters skim for the hazards and recommendations. Safely Track starts from your template, but the draft comes from what the inspector actually photographed and described, so each finding sits in the right section with its recommendation next to it.

The typical write-up
  • ✕ Notes and photos held until the inspector is back at a desk
  • ✕ Every template section filled by hand, relevant or not
  • ✕ Recommendation language retyped for each hazard
  • ✕ Policyholder follow-up by email with no proof of the fix
With Safely Track
  • ✓ Narrate the building as you walk it and tick the photos that show a hazard
  • ✓ Construction, occupancy and conditions land in your template's sections
  • ✓ Standard recommendation language drafted per hazard, for review
  • ✓ Policyholder closes the recommendation from a text link with a photo

How voice and photos feed the report.

Open a report and pick your survey template. Choose the policyholder and the property, drop in the survey photos, and talk through the building in the order you already inspect it. Hit generate: the voice memo and photos become a draft in your template, and each hazard gets standard recommendation language your underwriters already expect. The inspector reviews, edits and submits. You can watch this in the loss control demo video.

Which to choose

An honest recommendation.

Choose LC360 if

  • You are an insurer running a survey program across vendors and regions
  • You need property risk insight to decide which locations to survey
  • Your priority is the system of record, not the write-up

Look at Safely Track if

  • Your inspectors spend longer writing the report than walking the site
  • Underwriters skip most of each report to find the recommendations
  • You want it alongside your system of record, not instead of it
Questions

What loss control teams ask.

Is LC360 the same as Majesco Loss Control?

LossControl360 was built by Utilant, which Majesco acquired. Majesco now presents the product on its own site as Majesco Loss Control, alongside a field capture product called Guide Stream.

What does LC360 do that Safely Track doesn't?

Majesco Loss Control is a survey management platform for P&C insurers: it lists embedded property risk insight to prioritize surveys, automated assignment and reminders across field teams and vendors, self-surveys, and portfolio and reinsurance analysis. Safely Track does not offer property risk scoring or an insurer core-system suite.

Do I have to replace LC360 to use Safely Track?

No. Safely Track can run alongside your system of record. It handles the field capture and the report, and the finished report goes wherever your survey workflow needs it.

Why are loss control reports so long?

Many survey templates grew one section at a time, and every section gets filled whether the underwriter needs it or not. Safely Track builds the report from your template but drafts only what the inspector saw and said, so findings and recommendations are easy to find.

Can inspectors dictate a loss control survey report?

Yes. In Safely Track the inspector talks through the building while walking it and adds photos. The voice memo and photos are drafted into the sections of your survey template, with recommendation language for each hazard, and the inspector reviews before sending.

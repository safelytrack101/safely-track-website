Privacy Policy

The short version

We collect what you give us on this site, plus what your company puts into the Safely Track app to run its safety work. We use it to provide the service and to talk with you about it. We don't sell personal information, and we don't share phone numbers with anyone for their marketing. Email thomas@usesafelytrack.com with any question or request.

This policy covers Safely Track, LLC ("Safely Track," "we," "us"), the website at usesafelytrack.com, and the Safely Track application at app.usesafelytrack.com, including the links we send by text and email so people can respond to a corrective action without an account (together, the "Service").

Who controls the data

Two different situations apply, and the rules differ between them.

Our website and our own customer relationships. When you fill out a form on this site, book a call, or sign up for an account, Safely Track decides how that information is used. This policy governs it directly.

Data your organization puts into the app. Safety consulting firms, loss control teams and in-house safety teams use Safely Track to store site reports, photos, corrective actions, training records and similar records. That organization is our customer, and it controls that data. We process it on the customer's behalf under our agreement with them. If you are a worker, subcontractor, or client contact whose details appear in a customer's records, please send privacy requests to that organization first. We will help them respond.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Forms on this site. Name, work email, phone number, company, job title, and anything you type into a message field when you request a demo, a pilot, a guide, the whitepaper, or the AI readiness quiz results. Quiz answers are sent with the request.
  • Booking a call. When you book through Calendly, Calendly collects your name, email and the details you enter, and shares them with us.
  • Account information. Name, email, phone number, role, company, password (stored hashed), and settings for people who use the app.
  • Content in the app. Inspection and site walk reports, photos, voice memos and their transcripts, hazard observations, corrective actions and proof-of-fix photos, signatures, timesheets, schedules, training attendance and quiz results, consultant credentials and expiry dates, certificates of insurance, contracts, proposals and invoices. These records can include names, phone numbers and email addresses of workers, subcontractors and client contacts.
  • Safety Bench. Safety professionals who list themselves provide their name, contact details, location, availability, and the certifications they report. Firms who search it provide their contact details.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us by email, text or phone.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data. IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, referring page, and timestamps, recorded by our hosting providers when you load the site or the app.
  • Referral source. If you arrive at the site from an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, the site notes the assistant's name in your browser's session storage for that visit and includes it with any form you submit. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Analytics. The site is set up for Google Analytics 4. When it is on, Google sets cookies and records the pages you view and the buttons you click, such as "Book a demo." Google's use of that data is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • App data. When you use the app, we record actions such as sign-ins, report edits and link opens, for security, audit history and support. Photos you upload may carry the time and location they were taken.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: build reports, send corrective action links, track completion, store records and let your organization see them.
  • To draft content with AI. Photos, voice memos and notes you submit are processed by AI models to draft observation descriptions, cite standards, transcribe audio and build training material. A person at your organization reviews and approves what is published.
  • To reply to inquiries, run demos and pilots, and send the guide or report you asked for. We may follow up about Safely Track. You can opt out of follow-up email at any time.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, keep audit trails and troubleshoot problems.
  • To improve the site and the product, using usage data about how features are used.
  • To bill customers and keep business records.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not use customer content to train AI models for anyone else's benefit, and we do not allow the AI providers we use to train their models on it.

Text messages

The app can send text messages, for example a link asking a foreman or subcontractor to submit a photo once a hazard is fixed. Those texts are sent at the request of the customer organization. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts from that number, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with the service providers that deliver the messages for us.

How we share it

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only in these cases:

  • Within your organization's workspace. Records in the app are visible to the people your organization gives access, including its clients through the client portal.
  • With service providers who host, process or deliver data for us under contracts that limit their use of it. The categories are listed below.
  • For legal reasons, when we believe the law, a court order or a valid legal process requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.
  • In a business transfer. If Safely Track is acquired or merges, personal information may move to the new owner, which will remain bound by this policy for data collected under it.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Cookies

The app uses cookies to keep you signed in. The website does not set its own cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies when it is on, and Calendly may set cookies when you open the booking page. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them will sign you out of the app.

How long we keep it

  • Website inquiries are kept while we are in contact with you about Safely Track, and deleted on request.
  • Customer content is kept for as long as the customer's account is active, or as the customer directs. Safety records often need to be kept for years, so each customer decides how long to retain them. After an account ends, we delete or return customer content as the agreement provides, except where the law requires us to keep it. Copies in backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • Billing and tax records are kept as long as the law requires.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Access to production systems is limited to staff who need it and requires multi-factor authentication. Each customer's records are separated from every other customer's. We are working toward SOC 2 Type II, and our Trust Center describes our security program. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the affected customer as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or give you a copy. You can also unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any of them. Email thomas@usesafelytrack.com to make a request. We will confirm your identity first and reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU and UK GDPR may give you further rights, including the right to object to some processing and to complain to a data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our contract with you or your employer, our legitimate interest in running and marketing a business service, your consent, or a legal obligation, depending on the use.

If your information is in a customer's workspace, we will pass your request to that customer, since they control the record.

Where data is stored

Safely Track is based in the United States, and data is stored and processed in the United States. If you are outside the U.S., your information will be transferred there. Where the law requires it, we use standard contractual clauses or a similar safeguard for that transfer.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to other companies' websites, including the competitors we compare ourselves with. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use personal information we already hold, we will tell customers by email or in the app before it takes effect.

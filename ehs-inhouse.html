Contractor Corrective Actions

Assign it. Prove it got fixed. No login required for contractors.

Spot a hazard, assign the corrective action to the responsible contractor or crew via SMS, they receive a text with the finding and a link to upload proof of fix. No app, no onboarding, no training required on their end.

The closeout record is automatically timestamped and stored. When OSHA asks for proof, you pull it in seconds, not from someone's inbox.

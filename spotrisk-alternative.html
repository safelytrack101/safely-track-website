SpotRisk LossControlSoftware vs Safely Track

SpotRisk's LossControlSoftware describes itself as "inspection software for commercial insurance loss control." It organizes the work around the report. Safely Track organizes the same work (queue, assignments, review) and then does the part that eats the afternoon: it drafts the report from what the inspector said and photographed on the walk.

The workflow

Assignments and review are table stakes.

SpotRisk leads with assignment management and configurable forms. Safely Track has both, and adds the field side:

Request queue by due date

Survey requests land in one queue with the nearest due date on top. Assign each to an inspector and a time; two schedulers can never book the same one twice.

Due dates and overdue

Every survey carries its due date. One view shows what is overdue, what is due this week and your on-time rate.

Routes and confirmations

Each inspector's day is ordered to finish earliest, with visit lengths learned from past surveys. The site contact confirms by text, and reminders go out before the visit.

Review, then closeout

Reports wait in a review queue before they reach the client. The policyholder then closes each recommendation from a text link with a photo, no login.

What SpotRisk has that we don't.

Guided self-inspections for policyholders today; Safely Track's no-login self-survey link is being built now. SpotRisk also runs two side businesses: Loss Control Academy for inspector training and RecruitInspectors, a profile tool for people exploring inspection work.

Where they differ

Questionnaires, or the walk.

SpotRisk collects questionnaire answers and photos and assembles them into a report. Someone still has to answer every question. Safely Track starts from the walk instead. The inspector narrates the building while walking it, ticks the photos that show a hazard, and the report is drafted into your template with recommendation language for each finding, ready for review before they are back in the car.

Which to choose

Which one fits.

Choose SpotRisk if

  • You need guided policyholder self-inspections in production today
  • You want inspector training or recruiting from the same vendor

Look at Safely Track if

  • You want the queue, routes, review and the report in one place
  • Your inspectors lose an afternoon writing up each visit
  • Each carrier or client wants the report in its own format
  • You want policyholders to close recommendations with photo proof
Questions

What inspection firms ask.

What is SpotRisk LossControlSoftware?

LossControlSoftware is a SpotRisk product that describes itself as inspection software for commercial insurance loss control. It helps inspection companies, carriers and loss-control teams organize assignments, collect field or self-inspection data and assemble reports.

What does SpotRisk offer that Safely Track doesn't?

Guided self-inspections for policyholders, which Safely Track is building now, plus two side businesses: Loss Control Academy for inspector training and RecruitInspectors for people exploring inspection work. Assignments, reviewer workflow and private label are in both.

Which is better for independent loss control consultants?

Both coordinate assignments and reviewers. The difference is the hours after the walk: SpotRisk assembles a report from questionnaire answers, and Safely Track drafts it from your voice memo and photos in your template. For a solo consultant, Safely Track also plans the day route and texts the site contact to confirm.

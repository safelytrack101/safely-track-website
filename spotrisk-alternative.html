SpotRisk LossControlSoftware vs Safely Track

SpotRisk's LossControlSoftware describes itself as "inspection software for commercial insurance loss control," for inspection companies, carriers and loss-control teams. Safely Track serves a similar audience with a different center of gravity: the report the inspector writes after the walk. Here is a fair comparison on report creation from the field, report format control, and pricing transparency.

Credit where it's due

What SpotRisk offers.

Assignment management

Contacts, inspection instructions, due dates and status visible to schedulers, inspectors, reviewers and managers.

Configurable forms

Forms for different inspection types, client requirements, occupancy classes and review needs, with recommendation libraries.

Private label

Teams can run LossControlSoftware behind their own client experience, and field, remote and guided self-inspections are supported.

Training and recruiting

SpotRisk also runs Loss Control Academy for inspector training and certification, and RecruitInspectors, a profile tool for people exploring inspection work.

Where they differ

Workflow first, or the report first.

SpotRisk's site centers on organizing inspection work: intake, assignments, mobile-friendly questionnaires and photo collection, then bringing responses, images, notes and recommendations into structured reports for client delivery. Safely Track centers on the write-up itself. The inspector narrates the building while walking it, ticks the photos that show a hazard, and the report is drafted into your template with recommendation language for each finding, ready for review before they are back in the car.

Which to choose

An honest recommendation.

Choose SpotRisk if

  • Your hardest problem is coordinating assignments, inspectors and reviewers
  • You need guided self-inspections from policyholders
  • You also want inspector training or recruiting from the same vendor

Look at Safely Track if

  • Your inspectors lose an afternoon writing up each visit
  • Each carrier or client wants the report in its own format
  • You want policyholders to close recommendations with photo proof
Questions

What inspection firms ask.

What is SpotRisk LossControlSoftware?

LossControlSoftware is a SpotRisk product that describes itself as inspection software for commercial insurance loss control. It helps inspection companies, carriers and loss-control teams organize assignments, collect field or self-inspection data and assemble reports.

Does SpotRisk publish its pricing?

No pricing appears on the LossControlSoftware site. Its calls to action are a walkthrough request and an email to the SpotRisk team. Safely Track does not publish pricing either; it is quoted after a demo on your own reports.

What does SpotRisk offer that Safely Track doesn't?

SpotRisk lists assignment coordination for schedulers, inspectors and reviewers, guided self-inspections for policyholders, and a private-label option. SpotRisk also runs Loss Control Academy for inspector training and RecruitInspectors for people exploring inspection work. Safely Track has no inspector training academy or recruiting profile product.

Which is better for independent loss control consultants?

If you mainly need to coordinate a queue of assignments and reviewers, SpotRisk is built around that workflow. If your bottleneck is writing each survey report after the walk, Safely Track drafts it from your voice memo and photos in your template.

The problem

Short-notice safety coverage still runs on a phone tree.

A shutdown gets moved up. A client adds a second shift. A site safety rep gives notice two weeks into a six-month job. Whoever has to fill the gap starts making calls: the techs who worked the last job, a friend at another firm, a LinkedIn search for "CHST" plus a city name. Half the people don't pick up. Of the ones who do, some are booked, some don't hold the certification the client asked for, and some moved two states away last spring.

Meanwhile the good independent safety people are hard to find on purpose. They don't want their phone number on a job board, and they don't want a recruiter adding a markup to their rate. So they wait for the call from someone who already knows them.

For safety professionals
Free, always

Get found for the work you want. Keep your number to yourself.

If you work contract, per diem, travel, or you're open to the right full-time role, Safety Bench puts you in front of the firms looking for someone like you. You don't pay for it, ever, and nobody takes a cut of your rate.

  1. Fill in one form

    Your headline, years in EHS, certifications (CSP, CHST, OHST, OSHA 500 and 510, competent person cards, HAZWOPER and more), the industries and specialties you work in, your home city and state, whether you travel, when you're next free, and the hourly rate range you work for. No password to make up.

  2. Confirm from your email

    We email you a private link. Your listing stays hidden until you open it once. After that, the same link is how you update your availability, hide your listing for a while, or remove it.

    When a firm wants to talk, you get an email with who they are, the role, and their note. Accept and pick what to share: email, phone, LinkedIn, or any mix. Decline with a note if you like, or block that firm altogether.

What it isn't

Not a staffing agency, and not a job board.

We don't recruit, screen or place anyone, and we don't take a percentage of the hire or the rate. Safety Bench makes the introduction. The firm and the professional agree the terms between themselves. Certifications are self-reported today, so check them before you place someone, the same as you would with a resume.

Questions

What people ask first.

What does Safety Bench cost?

Listing yourself as a safety professional is free, and stays free. Hiring firms pay a flat monthly subscription. There are no placement fees and no cut of anyone's rate.

Do I need an account or a password to list myself?

No. You fill in one form and we email you a private link. That link is how you edit your listing, hide it, or remove it. Your listing stays hidden until you open the link once.

What can a firm see about me before I accept?

Your first name and last initial, your headline and summary, certifications, industries, specializations, city and state, whether you travel, when you're available, and a range around your hourly rate rather than the rate itself. Your email, phone, LinkedIn and full last name stay private until you accept, and you choose which of those to share.

Are certifications verified?

Not yet. Certifications on Safety Bench are self-reported, and firms see them labelled that way. Check credentials before you place someone, the same as you would with any resume.

Who gets a staffing call-out text?

Only the people on your own roster in Safely Track. Call-outs go by text, with email as the fallback, and nobody on the wider bench is contacted. Anyone can reply STOP.

We're an in-house safety team, not a staffing firm. Is this for us?

Yes. If you cover projects, turnarounds or leaves with contract safety people, you're hiring from the same pool consulting firms are. The subscription and the tools are the same.

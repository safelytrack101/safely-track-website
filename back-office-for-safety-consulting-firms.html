The problem

The office work grows with every person you put in the field.

Each new placement adds a line to the schedule, a timesheet to chase, a credential to watch, and a line on an invoice. Most firms run all of it on one office manager, or on the principal's evenings. When that person is out, moves on, or the firm grows, it backs up: a late invoice, a consultant sent to a site on an expired card, a Friday schedule that goes out on Sunday.

Software helps, but somebody still has to run it every week. That is the part we take on.

How it works

Software you don't have to run, with a person behind it.

The work runs on Safely Track, the same system consulting firms use for field reports, staffing, timesheets and billing. A named coordinator on our side runs your calendar each week and is the person you call.

As more of the work runs on its own, your price does not go up for it. You are paying for the deadlines, not for the hours behind them.

Questions

What firm owners ask first.

Do we have to let anyone go?

No. Most firms come to us when the office work outgrows the person doing it, or when that person retires or moves on. If you have an office manager, they spend their time on the work that needs judgment instead of chasing timesheets.

Our schedules are in Word and our timesheets are scans. Can you work with that?

Yes. Send them as they are. Getting them into shape is part of onboarding, and we show you anything that does not add up before we run a week on our own.

What happens if you miss a deadline?

That month's fee drops by 10%, and you hear it from us before you notice it yourself.

Who sees our client data?

Your coordinator and the Safely Track team that supports them. Your data sits in your own account, kept separate from every other firm's.

Do we have to use Safely Track for field reports too?

No. The back office runs on its own. If you want field reports, proof of fix or client portals later, it is the same system: see software for safety consulting firms.

How big does a firm need to be?

It is built for firms with roughly 10 to 100 people in the field. Below that, the software on its own is usually enough.

How is it priced?

Founding-firm pricing starts at $2,000 a month. The fee scales with the number of people you schedule in the field, not with logins, and it includes the software. A one-time onboarding fee covers loading and cleaning your data. The first 90 days are month to month.

Can we leave?

Yes. The first 90 days are month to month, and your data is yours to export at any time.

Tell us what your office does each week.

Thirty minutes. Bring last week's schedule or a timesheet, in whatever shape it is in, and we'll show you how that week would run.

The problem

Many sites, one checklist, and findings that don't get fixed.

Most multi-site inspection programs fail in the same places. None of them is about the checklist itself.

What usually happens
  • ✕ The same checklist runs at every location, and nobody compares the results across them
  • ✕ A finding gets logged, then waits on a manager who never opens the app
  • ✕ The report is long, goes out by email, and is read by no one
  • ✕ Staff training cards and certificates live in a binder at each site
What Safely Track does
  • ✓ Every location's findings in one record, filterable by site, issue type and date
  • ✓ Each corrective action goes to its owner by text or email, closed with a photo, no login
  • ✓ A report built from your own checklist, with the photos and corrective actions in it
  • ✓ Certifications tracked per person, with expiry flagged before a card lapses
What it does

What Safely Track does for multi-site teams.

Inspections from photos and voice notes

Walk the site, take photos, and talk through what you see on any phone. The finding and corrective action are drafted for you to review and edit, so nobody types up notes afterward. Capture works with no signal and syncs later.

Your checklists, not a library

Send the checklists and forms you use today. They are built as templates, so each location sees familiar paperwork on day one.

Corrective actions to closeout

The person responsible gets a link by text or email, replies with a photo of the fix, and the record closes with a name and a timestamp.

Certifications and training

Certifications tracked per person with expiry flagged. Turn the PowerPoint training you already teach into narrated online courses with completion tracking.

QR sign-in that only works on site

Scan a QR code and sign in on any phone with no app. Pin the location and set a radius, so a sign-in link texted to someone at home is flagged or refused.

Ask questions about your data

Ask in plain English across every location, such as which sites have open corrective actions, instead of exporting to Excel.

Most of this is described in more detail on the in-house safety software page.

How it works

Three common walk-throughs.

A retail store walk

A district manager walks a store on the usual checklist. At the back of the store they photograph a blocked fire exit and a stack of overloaded shelving and say what they see. Each finding is drafted with a corrective action. The blocked exit goes to the store manager by text. The store manager clears it, replies with a photo, and the item closes with their name and the time. The district manager sees which stores still have open items without calling around.

A hotel or restaurant safety check

A general manager runs the monthly safety check from the property's own form. In the kitchen, an extinguisher tag is out of date and the hood is overdue for cleaning; both get a photo and a voice note. The extinguisher goes to maintenance with a due date. Before the next shift, staff sign in to a short safety briefing by scanning a QR code, and the sign-in sheet is ready as a PDF.

A facilities inspection

A facilities lead inspects several buildings in a week. Findings like a damaged stair nosing or a missing ceiling tile in an electrical room are assigned to the vendor who does the repair, by text, with no account needed on their end. At the end of the month the lead asks which buildings still have open corrective actions and gets the list in seconds.

Questions

What multi-site teams ask first.

Can we use the checklists we already have?

Yes. Send the checklists and forms you use today and they are built as templates, so each location sees familiar paperwork on day one.

Do store, hotel or building staff need an app?

Inspectors use any phone. The people who fix a finding only need the text or email link they receive, and close it with a photo. No app download or login on their end.

Does it work in basements, back rooms and other places with no signal?

Field capture works with no signal. Photos, notes and reports are saved on the phone and sync when it is back online. AI drafting runs once the phone is connected.

Is Safely Track only for construction?

No. Inspections, corrective actions, sign-in and training work the same way for stores, hotels, restaurants and buildings as they do for construction sites and plants.

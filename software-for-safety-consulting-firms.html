Software for safety consulting firms: two tools or one system?

Many firms run field inspections in SafetyCulture and the business side in monday.com. That pairing can work. This page is an honest look at when it does, when one system built for consulting firms fits better, and what each option costs today.

The short answer

Two good tools, one gap between them.

SafetyCulture handles field inspections well, and monday.com handles general project tracking well. Firms that run field delivery and the business side in two tools end up entering the same clients, projects and people in both. Safely Track is built for safety consulting firms specifically, and keeps field reports, staffing and the client record in one place.

Credit where it's due

When SafetyCulture + monday.com is the better choice.

Mature, widely used products

Both are established tools many safety professionals and office staff already know, with mobile apps on iOS and Android.

Templates and integrations

SafetyCulture's Premium plan lists unlimited inspection templates and out-of-the-box integrations. monday.com lists 200+ templates and integrations on its paid plans.

Free tiers for small teams

SafetyCulture's free plan covers up to 10 seats and 5 active inspection templates. monday.com's free plan covers up to 2 seats.

You only need checklists

If clients accept a standard inspection export and the business side is simple, a checklist app plus a board is a cheap, flexible setup.

Questions

What consulting firms ask.

What's the best software for a small safety consulting firm?

It depends on what the firm delivers. If you mostly run checklists and clients accept a standard export, SafetyCulture's free plan (up to 10 seats) may be enough. If each client wants its own report, you chase fixes with client contacts, and you track hours and certifications for the same people, a system built for consulting firms, such as Safely Track, saves the re-entry between tools.

Can SafetyCulture handle multiple clients?

SafetyCulture (now presented as Mitti) is built for frontline teams inspecting their own sites. Its Premium plan lists custom branded reports and free guest seats, which help with outside users. Firms with many clients usually still manage each client's report format, closeout follow-up and billing outside it.

Is there an alternative to SafetyCulture for safety consultants?

Yes. Safely Track is built for safety consulting firms: field reports in each client's format, hazards closed out by the client contact from a text or email link, and consultant certifications, timesheets and invoicing in the same system. Other tools consultants compare include SiteDocs and Safety Reports.

How do safety consultants track employee certifications?

Many firms use a spreadsheet or a general tool like monday.com, which means a second copy of their people list. In Safely Track, certifications sit on the same person record that files the reports, so you can see who is qualified for a client's site before you send them.

Do I need monday.com if I already use SafetyCulture?

Only if you need project tracking SafetyCulture does not cover. monday.com is a general work management tool, so the firm sets up its own boards for clients, projects and staff, and those records do not sync to the inspection app on their own.

How much does safety consulting software cost?

SafetyCulture lists Premium at $24 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 monthly. monday.com lists Basic, Standard and Pro at $9, $12 and $19 per seat per month billed annually. Safely Track does not publish pricing; it is quoted per firm after a demo.

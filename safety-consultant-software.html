Why the usual tools don't fit

In-house EHS software answers the wrong question for a consultant.

Inspection apps and enterprise EHS suites are good at what they were built for: one company inspecting its own sites. A consulting firm's problem is different. You are producing a deliverable for someone else, many times a week, across clients who each want it their own way, and you have to staff, prove, and bill that work.

What it does

The consulting work, from the site walk to the invoice.

Most firms start with reporting, because that is where the hours most obviously go. The rest is there when you need it.

Field capture in the walk

Photos and a voice note on any phone. The finding, the corrective action, and the likely OSHA standard are drafted for the consultant to review and edit.

Each client's report format

Templates are built from the forms you already use. Daily reports fill from the day's observations instead of being re-typed.

Weekly and monthly summaries

The week's daily reports pull into a draft client summary. The consultant edits and sends it.

Proof of fix, no login needed

The person responsible gets a link by text or email, replies with a photo, and the record closes with a timestamp.

Consultant credentials

Certifications tracked per person, with expiry flagged before a card lapses and a view of who is qualified for which client.

Staffing, hours, and billing

Schedules, timesheets, and hours by project, feeding client invoices and margin per client.

A portal under your brand

Clients see their findings and trends between visits, with your firm's name on it.

One searchable record

Every site, date, contractor, and issue type across all clients, filterable in seconds instead of opened file by file.

In practice

One firm, more than 60 safety managers, more than 70 sites.

One of our customers is a safety consulting firm that places full-time safety managers on clients' manufacturing plants and construction sites. Before Safely Track, every manager captured observations, inspections, and incidents a different way: paper notes written up hours later, photos that stayed on a phone, a spreadsheet on one laptop, hazards raised in a group text with no owner. Each week someone read every daily report and wrote the client summary from scratch.

With Safely Track

Questions

What consulting firms ask first.

How is this different from regular EHS software?

Regular EHS software assumes one employer with one safety department. A consulting firm has many clients, each with its own report format, credential requirements, contacts, and invoice. Safely Track keeps each client separate while giving the firm one view across all of them, and it covers the business side too: staffing, hours, and billing.

Can reports go out in each client's format and under our brand?

Yes. Templates are built from the forms you already use, so each client gets the report it already expects, with your firm's logo. Clients can also see their findings in a portal under your branding.

Do client workers or subcontractors need an account?

No. The person responsible for a fix gets a text or email with a link, replies with a photo, and the record closes with a timestamp. No app download or login on their end.

How long does it take to get running?

Send the forms you use today and they get built as templates, so the field team sees familiar paperwork on day one. The firm described above replaced its previous platform within three weeks of going live.

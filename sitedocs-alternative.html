Why firms compare

Why consulting firms look at alternatives.

When a safety consulting firm starts comparing software, it is usually for one of three reasons, whatever tool it uses today.

Cost and terms that grow with the firm

Subscriptions priced by headcount or company size, renewed annually, cost more with every consultant you hire, while the client work is what you actually sell.

Built for one company's program

Most safety platforms are designed around one employer running its own safety program. A firm serves many clients, each with its own report format, contacts and requirements.

The back office lives in spreadsheets

The inspection gets done in the app, but hours, invoices and margin per client are still in spreadsheets and QuickBooks.

The report is still rebuilt by hand

Field notes still get rewritten into each client's deliverable and weekly summary, which is where most consultant hours go.

Credit where it's due

What SiteDocs does well.

Broad construction safety coverage

Digital forms including scheduled and recurring ones, inspections, incidents, hazards, corrective actions, orientations, contractor and equipment management.

Works offline on any device

SiteDocs says its mobile app works fully offline on iOS, Android and Amazon devices.

Certifications and workers

Worker profiles and certification tracking with expiry alerts, and logins for all workers included in the subscription.

A program for consultants

A Safety Consultant Program with visibility across client job sites and revenue sharing, plus integrations including Procore.

Questions

What firms comparing SiteDocs ask.

Who owns SiteDocs?

SiteDocs says it is part of the Nemetschek Group, which it joined in July 2024 through the acquisition of GoCanvas.

Is SiteDocs a good fit for a safety consulting firm?

For running forms and a contractor's own safety program, often yes, and it has a consultant program. Firms tend to look further when they need each client's own report format, weekly client summaries, closeout from people outside the system, and hours and billing per client in the same place.

Does SiteDocs publish its pricing?

No. Its pricing page describes an annual subscription based on company size, with a quote after a call.

Can we move our existing forms into Safely Track?

Yes. Send the forms and reports you use today and they are built as templates, so your team sees familiar paperwork on day one.

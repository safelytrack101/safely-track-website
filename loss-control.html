Built For The Walk-Through

Narrate the building as you walk it. Skip re-typing it later.

Exterior, common areas, then utilities, the same order you already inspect in. Instead of holding it all in your head until you're home, talk through it as you go, and let AI organize the photos and structure the write-up.

→ "Front view, next." Photos label and order themselves as you shoot them, no manual sorting back at the desk.
→ "Masonry construction, three stories, six units, two per floor." Construction type and occupancy details flow straight into the report.
→ "Electrical panel, basement, older breakers, evidence of overheating." AI drafts the finding and flags it as a hazard needing a recommendation.
→ "Water heater leaking, corroded plumbing." Standard recommendation language gets suggested automatically, ready to review.

Your twenty-five minute walk stays twenty-five minutes. The report that used to eat your afternoon is mostly done before you're back in the car.
